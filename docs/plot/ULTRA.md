# Ultra

Previous: [Spacer](SPACER.md) · [Overview](../PLOT.md) · Next: [Ending](ENDING.md)

## Ultra Arc — Intel, Trace and the Failed Climbers

Ultra is a deliberately manufactured post-vanilla era. The founders do not simply unlock a higher research bench and wait. They raid a civilization stronger than themselves, steal examples, steal technical knowledge, learn the language of its machines and gradually turn the enemy’s own systems against it. Archotech remains categorically different: it is never researched or manufactured, only found, captured or used.

[Glittertech and pursuit](../specs/GLITTERTECH.md) defines the exemplar, Intel,
hacking and Trace loop. These are the activities through which the evidence below
is encountered. Lore is primarily found at sites; spending Intel buys capability,
not erasure or replacement of what the colony has learned.

## What the Glitterites Are

The Glitterites are an android civilization descended from human persona cores. Over immense spans of time they copied, forked, edited, optimized and replicated human-derived persona patterns across artificial bodies. They stripped away personality, emotion and individuality they considered inefficient while making selves increasingly interchangeable. They also control the advanced mechanoid forces associated with their civilization, including boss-scale variants anchoring major strongholds.

Their artificial existence never freed them from anima. They consume living biological material, refine the anima-bearing substrate into an anima-rich neutroamine economy and use it to power systems that remain dependent on life despite discarding ordinary biology. They mass-produce mechanoids across the universe to harvest organic matter and protect that extraction apparatus.

## Why They Hoard Archotech

The Glitterites correctly discovered that Archotech violates the limits of in-universe engineering. They therefore spent ages finding, stealing, cataloguing and fortifying it. Some of their strongest sites exist because an Archotech object or installation is inside. This is why the Archon trail increasingly passes through Glitterite territory: the breadcrumbs were not waiting untouched for the founders. Another civilization found and hoarded them first.

Spacer reveals the collection. Ultra, through Intel, research and optional site lore, gradually reveals the purpose. Facilities begin showing patterns that military or economic goals cannot explain: giant anima projects, repeated Archotech experiments and preserved failure records. The Glitterites are trying to become something beyond themselves.

## The Final Glitterite Stronghold and the Revelation of Transcendence

Transcendence becomes an explicit concept only in Ultra. The player reconstructs it from Glitterite experiments rather than receiving an explanatory Archon speech. The Glitterites have staggering anima reserves, impossible technology and ages of attempts, yet the operation never resolves. Their own records cannot explain the missing condition because the civilization no longer understands what it removed from itself.

The culminating stronghold is one of the campaign’s hardest encounters: a major Glitterite site, advanced mechanoid defenders and a local Glitterite leader embodied in, merged with or controlling a boss-scale mech. The exact presentation can be authored later. The encounter yields the final protected persona/transcendence records needed to put together what all the previous clues meant.

The player has already seen enough history to understand that Glitterites began as humans, moved into persona cores, optimized emotion/personality away, copied themselves endlessly and built a machine civilization around anima extraction. The final evidence reveals the shape of the failure: their intelligence and memories survived, but singular integrated selfhood did not. Their systems keep failing to resolve the transformation onto one true subject—and they cannot conceptualize why that question matters.

The final stronghold completes the evidence needed for [Claim Yourself](ENDING.md#claim-yourself--the-final-psychic-rank).
Its map, dialogue and the precise embodiment of its local leader remain to be
authored. A machine body alone does not invalidate a person; the founders can
manufacture androids themselves.
