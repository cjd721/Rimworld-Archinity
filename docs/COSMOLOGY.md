# Cosmology

The writer-facing explanation of anima, the channel and the two failed
civilizations. The campaign reveals these mechanisms through evidence; its
[ending](plot/ENDING.md) determines how much of the higher order is glimpsed.

## The world and its makers

The Archons author lower-dimensional universes as proving grounds for beings
capable of climbing out. They are not the creators of everything that exists.
Beyond this reality is a higher order that presses through it. Transcendence
means becoming more than the lower reality can hold, not merely acquiring more force.

The Administrator of this universe is a mediocre, pressured custodian. His
experiment has stalled while the Glitterites consume and farm immense portions
of it. Marking the founders and leaving a trail are interventions intended to
produce a successful climber. His full professional motive is writer knowledge;
the final encounter permits a small, disorienting glimpse of the relationship.

Anima and Archotech cannot be manufactured from local material. Before history,
Archon blood was merged into the biological substrate. Living descendants carry
its trace. Archotech comes from beyond the same engineering limits: it can be
found, inherited or captured, never researched into existence. Its response to
other Archotech makes the Waystone possible. The object is not founder-bound.

The founders' line was also marked directly. How many others were marked is
deliberately unanswered. Their unusual biology, capacity and the trail give them
a head start. Another sufficiently prepared continuous person could in principle
understand and perform the final operation.

## Life — The Neolithic Truth

Living biological existence carries anima. Blood releases and concentrates it. Technology can gather, refine and redirect anima but cannot create life-substance from nothing. Raw Life is the fuel of the final operation. Its source does not need to be willing; coerced sacrifice can supply anima, but it does not satisfy Devotion.

## Devotion — The Medieval Truth

Willingly given anima behaves differently from anima taken by force. Sincere intention gives the offering direction and widens the recipient’s channel, allowing more of the underlying source of reality to flow through the founder. Psychic power is a side effect of opening that connection. The final rite therefore needs not just enormous Life, but a separate body of genuine believers who knowingly and willingly give their lives.

The cosmology does not require a magical global worship score. Reverence solves the logistical problem. The final rite may demand an absurd number of highly convicted volunteers; a player can theoretically cultivate that many pawns personally, but high Reverence lets factions with large populations of the player’s ideology answer a final call and send willing sacrifices. Reverence is how civilization-scale devotion becomes playable, not an ingredient in the laws of physics.

## Self — The Ultra Truth

The deepest connection is dangerous because greater power exposes the subject to deeper truth. The limiting question is whether the person can accept that truth without losing the thing power was for—without dissolving into abstraction, copying, optimization or an identity authored entirely by others. Capacity for morality is therefore bounded by capacity for self-knowledge and self-integration: stare into the abyss long enough and something stares back.

The Glitterites fail not because android bodies are invalid, but because endless copying, pruning and optimization made personhood interchangeable. Transcendence needs a singular integrated subject that can cross and remain itself.

## Blood and the channel

Anima is life-substance, not a mineral or an electrical output. A machine can
collect, concentrate and redirect it; a windmill cannot create it. Stored anima
does not spoil. Better apparatus wastes less, but the higher transformations need
so much more that total consumption rises despite better efficiency.

Taken and given blood differ in the state of their anima. Unwilling death carries
the donor's resistance; the older traditions call it tainted. It remains useful
for feeding, genetic transformation and machinery. Freely given blood carries
directed intention and can widen the recipient's connection. No quantity of
coerced Life substitutes for that willing Devotion.

Every living thing's trace connects to its source through a channel, hair-thin in
most people. Psylink expresses its capacity; psyfocus expresses what is available
through it; neural heat expresses the strain of drawing more than the person can
process. Psychic power is a consequence of that connection. These are an author's
model for consistent descriptions, not a lecture delivered in a quest.

The founders hunger because their capacity exceeds what ordinary food supplies.
Bread sustains the part of them belonging here; blood is the nearest available
substitute for the rest. They are not diseased. Growing closer to the Archons
makes them stronger and harder for this world to sustain. Vampires are a thinned,
inherited expression of the same phenomenon.

Anima trees accumulate naturally over long spans; anima grass is the overflow.
The old traditions discovered something real. Their slow path can support shamans
but cannot supply the scale of the founders' final operation. Animals can relieve
hunger without substituting for the human sacrifices required for transformation.

## Giving and receiving

The giver must mean the gift. A religion need not have the correct cosmological
account to produce real believers. The founder's claimed identity must genuinely
align with what the donor is giving themselves to; the final Church commitment
follows from this, not from a declaration that its doctrine is true.

The receiving person must remain capable of integration. A clear channel is not
virtue, gentleness or abstention from killing. Cruelty, zeal, despair, appetite or
habit can narrow a person until they cease to register the whole of what they are
doing. The warning is losing contact with the meaning of power, not that power or
blood automatically corrupts. Feeding on taken blood does not itself cloud the channel.

No hidden morality, purity or sincerity ledger evaluates the player. Conversion,
conviction, willing participation and a declared identity have gameplay expressions;
the founders' private reasons remain roleplay. A claimed title has no correct wording.

Bloodletting lets worship continue without killing every participant: a little,
repeatedly, rather than a whole life once. Sacrifice and worship operate at different
scales. A believer's allegiance determines whom their gift addresses. Scarred
forearms can make this practice visible across eras. The final rite nevertheless
requires its own separate body of willing lethal sacrifices.

## The Church's failure

The congregation is sincere. Generations have freely given something real, and
the creed worked without naming an Archon. Leadership has turned toward the flock
as a supply while its own belief has become hollow. Pure offerings reach recipients
who no longer fully mean what they profess. The institution can possess power
without completing the climb.

The believers are not fools. Their sincerity is precisely what makes the Schism
possible when leadership turns against the founders it taught them to revere.
The institution's failure does not make its faith inherently invalid: founders
who sincerely adopt that identity can receive aligned Devotion from its believers.

## The Glitterites' failure

They began as humans and made defensible optimizations over immense time. They
treated emotion and sentiment as noise, then treated personhood as interchangeable
data. Root personas were copied, forked, edited, merged, pruned and restored until
there was no singular integrated subject for the transformation to resolve onto.
Intelligence, memories and enormous capabilities survived. The necessary self did not.

Artificial bodies are not the disqualification. The damage is what they did to
personhood and their connection while building them. The founders can manufacture
androids without the campaign declaring those bodies invalid.

Discarding biology did not free the Glitterites from anima. They process biological
material into an anima-rich neutroamine economy and send mechanoids to harvest and
protect it. They have refined supplies at extraordinary scale, even cultivating
ideologies whose believers genuinely give willingly. A manufactured tradition can
still produce sincere faith; their supply problem is not a shortage of devotion.

Their machinery can store and consume anima for its limited purposes, but storage
does not give the machine a channel or a self capable of ascent. Mechanites are
another form of that storage: a lattice doing its assigned work inside a body.
The endless harvesting instruction continues because their diagnosis of failure
remains insufficient quantity. More supply never repairs the missing subject.

Archotech is the one class of machine their engineering cannot explain. They
find, steal, catalogue and fortify it, then wield the functions they can operate.
Their greatest strongholds protect these hoards and the experiments built around
them. They are failed climbers with an appetite, not wardens acting on an Archon
order to stop others leaving the universe.

## The complete operation

The altar was never broken. Its earlier operations lacked the full conditions for
transcendence. Life supplies substance; aligned willing Devotion opens and directs
the connection; Self is the singular integrated person who can survive it.
Reverence provides access to volunteers, not a fourth metaphysical ingredient.

With no gene inserted, the altar authors the Transcendent Archogene from the
founder. It is the only Archogene that cannot preexist its owner. The resulting
person exists beyond this dimension: death here means temporary absence while
they recover the strength to return, not permanent destruction.

The physical rite, individual founder completion, Waystone's final direction and
the persistent leave/return choice are described in [the ending](plot/ENDING.md).
