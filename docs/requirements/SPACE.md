# Space — the gravship as home, and orbit

## Purpose

The gravship carries the campaign across the line where the planet stops being the
world. It arrives in late Industrial as extraordinary transport, becomes the colony's
permanent home through Spacer, and is the only home the colony has once orbit opens.
Orbit is the reset: planetary gods arrive as strangers among civilizations that have
lived above them for generations. [Spacer](../plot/SPACER.md), [Ultra](../plot/ULTRA.md)

## Meaning

**Home is the ship, not the tile.** The colony still lands, farms a place, builds
temporary structures and leaves. What makes the gravship home is that nothing of the
colony has to stay behind when it flies.

**The reveal is a curtain, not a discovery.** Orbit is not somewhere the player
gradually notices. Before the reveal it does not exist for them at all; after it, the
offworld board is there in full.

## Required behavior

### The gravship as home

- The gravship grows from a small, short-ranged transport into the colony's main base,
  gaining range, capacity, life support, food production, habitation, storage and
  defenses as it develops. **This is vanilla Odyssey's own arc plus Vanilla Gravship
  Expanded, and the campaign adopts it as shipped.**
- The colony must be able to live aboard permanently, with the planetside base retired.
  Retiring it is the player's choice; nothing forces the move.
- No population target is stated. Capacity grows with the ship, and sizing is playtest
  work rather than a requirement.
- Landing somewhere, working it and building temporary structures there remains ordinary
  play at every stage.
- **Founders receive total vacuum immunity through the story** — vanilla's archite gene
  does exactly this. Everyone else uses suits.
- A stranded or crippled ship is recovered by ordinary means, as vanilla already
  provides. The campaign adds no rescue system and states no new failure.

### Living in orbit

- **Ordinary colony life must continue on an orbital home** wherever the fiction allows
  it: people joining, traders, visitors, raids, quests, disasters that make sense in
  space. Planet-only events stay on the planet, and when the ship is landed the planet's
  events apply. How close this can get to vanilla's planetside variety is a capability
  question, not a promise.
- There are no caravans in orbit. The colony reaches places by ship and by shuttle, and
  nothing is found by travelling — natural discovery does not operate there
  ([Charting](CHARTING.md) § *Natural discovery*).
- **A jump between the planet and orbit, in either direction, always counts as a
  qualifying relocation** for Glitterite pursuit ([pressure](PRESSURE.md) §
  *Glitterite pursuit*).
- **A move between two places in orbit counts the same way, past a distance threshold.** As
  on the planet, a short move does not grant a fresh safe window. (Conrad, #118, 2026-09-23)

### The reveal

- **Before the reveal, orbit is completely closed**: no view of the orbital map, no
  flight to it, and no orbital sites of any kind.
- **Nothing in orbit makes contact with the colony** — a trader's hail, a signal, a site —
  **until the colony can reach or talk to orbit.** The stages by which that opens are routes.
  (Conrad, #118, 2026-09-23)
- **Planet-side sites that a grav engine makes reachable exist once the colony has a grav
  engine** — Odyssey's planet-side gravcore leads, the source of grav panels and parts. This
  is how the gravship arc is adopted "as shipped" alongside a closed orbit: the planet-side
  leads stay, and the orbital leads are what closing orbit holds shut. (Conrad, #118,
  2026-09-23)
- The reveal is a single campaign moment, earned as the reward of the planetary
  resolution. It happens once, for the world, and both founders share it.
- At the reveal the orbital map is visible and fully populated, exactly as the world map
  is: the spacer powers on their stations, every terrestrial institution the resolution
  carried upward, and the Glitterite settlements the other powers route around. The
  player can see what not to touch.
- Whether Glitterite settlements are hostile to everyone and undiplomatic is
  [Glittertech](GLITTERTECH.md)'s; their visibility at the reveal is stated here.

## Campaign progression

| Stage | What the ship is |
| --- | --- |
| Late Industrial | Extraordinary transport and a military platform, not a home. [Industrial](../plot/INDUSTRIAL.md) |
| Early Spacer | The decisive mobile power that finishes the planetary struggle. [Spacer](../plot/SPACER.md) |
| Spacer — Departure | Home. The planetside base can be retired; the reveal opens orbit. |
| Ultra | The thing being hunted. Relocation is the answer to pursuit, and the ship is the only exit. [Pressure](PRESSURE.md) |

## Player information and agency

- The player chooses when to retire the planetside base, and can keep it.
- The player chooses when to open orbit by pursuing the reward that lifts the curtain;
  the curtain never lifts by accident.
- Relocation distance must stay legible, so a short hop is an informed risk
  ([pressure](PRESSURE.md)). A layer change is unambiguously far enough.

## Constraints

- **Vanilla behavior is the path of least resistance and is preferred throughout.** Where
  vanilla already answers a question — stranding, ship growth, map lifetime — the
  campaign adopts it rather than fighting it.
- This document selects no mechanism. What unlocks the reveal, and what must be patched
  for orbit to stay alive, are capability questions below.
- The political resolution that decides *who* ascends is [politics](POLITICS.md)'.
