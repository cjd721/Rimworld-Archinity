# Archinity campaign plot

September 2026 · [Archived final source](archive/Archinity_Campaign_Plot_and_Systems_Map_FINAL.docx)

Two marked founders build a civilization from the Neolithic to orbit and beyond.
Life supplies anima; Devotion opens and directs the psychic channel; Self is the
singular, integrated person who can survive its deepest connection. The founders
assemble these truths while confronting civilizations that each mistook a partial
answer for the whole.

The player chooses the founders' ideology, loyalties and final identity. The world
supplies discoveries, temptations and consequences. The ordered Archon discoveries
remain available when the player is ready; faction politics and RimWorld's
simulation create the events around that spine.

## Campaign in play

The founders' lasting capabilities grow through earned discoveries. Standing can
fall: orbit makes them strangers, and the first Glitterite encounter exposes how
far they still have to climb. Losses, rescues and reversals emerge from play;
the campaign does not script colonist deaths or require a forced defeat.

There is no calendar race. Pressure responds to era, capability, faction standing,
Reverence, Trace and player action. Parking an era remains valid. The internal
balancing target is an average 10–12 in-game years, not a deadline the fiction
announces. Difficulty follows era and capability rather than colony wealth;
religious attention and Glitterite pursuit modify the world's response.

The run leaves things worth keeping: a medieval throne room, an old ally, a
religion with institutions beyond the colony. New technology offers new answers
to lasting needs. Food can reach its useful ceiling early; not every domain needs
a new rung every era. Specialist kits are assembled across research branches,
with enough separation that intermediate equipment gets used.

The first Neolithic answers to basic needs do not require expeditions. Later
meaningful leaps can require instruction or captured examples. Reliable acquisition
routes matter more than lucky rewards. Avoid warehouses of unusable mystery items,
chains of intermediate processing and compulsory busywork. Optimization should
produce the intended civilization, including a real congregation, rather than
reward suppressing wealth or endlessly repeating trivial tasks.

The campaign is written and playtested as single-player, then adapted to Multiplayer.
Its fiction does not require a co-op rewrite. Engineering still supports the shared
world and separate colonies described by the multiplayer tickets.

## Tone and revelation

Sacral and gothic with an edge of realism: Roman Catholic Church meets Vlad the
Impaler, moving into a dystopian machine civilization. The Archons are gods, not
void entities; demonic imagery belongs, eldritch horror does not. Donghua and manhwa
supply the climb and cultivation, the Book of Enoch the sacred otherness, and
The Matrix the feeling of discovering deeper rules. The universe is not confirmed
to be a computer simulation.

The opening invitation measures the founders: they can join a higher order, but
are nowhere near strong enough. Archon contact remains scarce: the unwitnessed
marking, the opening invitation, and the final encounter. The threshold encounter
is tracked persistently; individual founder ascension does not add a recurring
Archon quest giver.

Evidence reveals what happens and how. Testimony can become oral tradition,
institutional teaching, dispute and rediscovery without prescribing the founders'
creed. Other faiths need not share their account. The campaign does not grade
ideologies or score the player's sincerity. Refer to what their people believe
rather than putting scripture in the protagonists' mouths.

Use plain language for blood, sacrifice, belief and selfhood. Anima and cultivation
are established vocabulary; imported meridians, qi and similar terminology are
not. Explain gameplay clearly without turning the fictional channel into a
technical lecture. The final Administrator scene deliberately permits a small
glimpse of the proving ground and the sense of being scouted; it leaves the full
professional motive and Archon society mostly hidden.


## Full Playthrough Map at a Glance

| Phase | Primary plot beat | System emphasis | Player-facing question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prologue | Archon marks founders, triggers First Thought and leaves Waystone. | Capacity for civilization; Archotech direction without explanation. | Survive, learn and interpret the impossible object. |
| N1 Awakening | Star Table; first Archon return; altar discovered; charge without transformation. | Primitive Charting + altar mystery. | What is this machine accepting, and what is missing? |
| N2 Primitive | Permanent settlement; Genius Archogene. | Era-banded discovery and rapid learning. | Become capable of building a real settlement. |
| N3 Settled Tribe | Local map solved; optional anima-tree contact explains anima. | Mature primitive civilization; no psylink yet. | Finish the primitive game before religion/psychics explode the scope. |
| M1 Seduction | Church courts founders; heroic public deed introduces Reverence; willing-blood rite gives first psylink. | Exaltation/Titles, Reverence UI, Psychic Rank, Observatory. | Who owns the story of what you did? |
| M2 Tension | Player faith spreads; Global Reverence makes Church leadership increasingly wary; pilgrims/apostles begin mattering. | Goodwill vs Reverence; Church titles; living faction politics. | Do you let the institution define you or cultivate believers directly? |
| M3 Schism / War | Founder-believing insiders break; Church may betray; open conflict begins. | Church Exaltation OR Schism Influence, plus Reverence. | Stay sanctioned, overthrow the hierarchy, or build independently? |
| Industrial | Roads, vehicles, trade, genetics, outposts and religious institutions make the planet interconnected. | Genetic democratization; vassals/revolts; Observatory → Sensory Array. | Can your colony become a planetary network? |
| Late Industrial | Gravship acquired as superior transport; wars escalate. | Planetary mobility; ship development. | Can mobility become decisive political power? |
| Early Spacer — Crown | Use gravship to finish Church/Schism/independent conflict and tertiary wars. | Final planetary religious/political state. | Who follows humanity when you leave? |
| Spacer — Departure | Gravship becomes home; orbital-access gate obtained; orbit instantiated. | Chosen terrestrial institution follows. | Leave the castle and reveal the larger world. |
| Spacer — Small Again | Meet Trader’s Guild, Starjacks and new house-derived Spacer powers. | New diplomacy; new Reverence begins near zero. | What are planetary gods worth to civilizations that never heard of them? |
| Spacer — Forbidden Territory | Everyone warns against Glitterites; Waystone resolves return inside their space. | First terrifying Glitterite contact. | Is the next rung worth going where nobody sane goes? |
| Spacer Capstone | Deliberate Glitterite heist recovers Glitterheart/analytical key. | Best conventional gear vs post-vanilla enemy. | Can you steal enough fire to understand it? |
| Ultra I — Exemplars | Raid for hardware and technical artifacts before advanced research. | Surviving research exemplars + destructive Intel artifacts. | What do you need badly enough to attack them for? |
| Ultra II — Intrusion | Intel is converted into the instruction needed to unlock Glitterite protocol/hacking capabilities. | Remote hacking, destructive analysis and serious hacks create Trace. | Can you make their own systems obey you before they find you? |
| Ultra III — Anatomy | Lore and research reveal android/persona civilization, mechanoid control and anima economy. | Intel/Trace loop; android production. | What are these beings, and why do they still need life? |
| Ultra IV — Failed Climbers | Archotech experiments reveal transcendence; final stronghold exposes loss of integrated self. | Archon spine converges with Glitterite endgame. | What are they trying to become, and what did they remove from themselves? |
| Claim Yourself | Final psychic “?” becomes player-authored title. | Self as a ritual prerequisite, not an object. | After everyone else named you, who do you say you are? |
| Final Rite | Life + willing Devotion + Self converge; the altar grants the existing `VRE_Transcendent` gene. | Reverence supplies access to volunteer believers but is not itself a metaphysical ingredient. | Can you open the channel completely and remain yourself? |
| Threshold / Administrator | Waystone points out; transcendent founder meets Administrator. | Tiny glimpse behind the curtain. | Did you just pass someone else’s entrance exam? |
| Victory / Postgame | Enter the new reality and roll credits, or stay in this reality with the transcendent pawn unchanged and keep playing. | A vanilla ending remains available if the player stays. | Do you leave now, or finish becoming everything this universe allows? |
